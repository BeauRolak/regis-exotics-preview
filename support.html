Common questions

How much is the Escalade-V per day?

$700 per day, plus a separate $800 deposit. The estimator counts calendar days from pickup to return, with a one-day minimum.

How old do I need to be to rent?

You need to be 21 or older.

Do I need insurance?

Yes. Full insurance is required to rent.

Does sending an inquiry reserve the vehicle?

No. An inquiry is a request. Nothing is reserved until REGIS EXOTICS confirms availability and the final price with you.

How do I send my inquiry?

Plan your dates on this site, copy the prepared inquiry, then open @regisexotics on Instagram and paste it into a message. You send the message yourself.

What do the Ram TRX trucks cost per day?

The white 2024 TRX is $460 for a one-day rental, $430 per day for two days, and $390 per day for three days or longer. For a weekly or monthly rental, message @regisexotics directly. The rate for the blue TRX is awaiting confirmation.
